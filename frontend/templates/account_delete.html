Delete your DocuElevate account

The self-service deletion flow is not available yet.

Until it is released, request deletion by emailing docuelevate@christian-louis.de. Include the account email address, but never include a password or document content.

What will be handled

The deletion request covers the DocuElevate account, account profile, stored source documents, OCR output, document metadata and configured integration credentials. Copies already written to a connected provider are managed under that provider's controls and may need separate removal there. We will review connected grants and queued processing as part of the completed deletion procedure.

Current process

This is an interim, manual process. We will confirm the request and handle deletion under the privacy notice; self-service deletion is not available in the app yet.
